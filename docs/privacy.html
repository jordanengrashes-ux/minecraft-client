Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Voxel Client ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Voxel Client desktop application and associated website (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This document is written plainly so you understand exactly what we do and do not do with your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the Service. This includes:

  • Account credentials: If you create a Voxel Client account (username and password), your credentials are stored securely using Firebase Authentication provided by Google. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Microsoft / Mojang authentication tokens: When you log in with your Microsoft account to play Minecraft, authentication tokens are stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit or store these tokens on our servers.
  • Display name and profile data: Your chosen display name, XP, rank, and cosmetics preferences are stored in Firebase Firestore to enable features such as leaderboards, friends, and cosmetics.
  • Chat messages: Messages sent in the Global Chat feature are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. Messages may be retained for moderation purposes.
  • Friends list and social data: Friend relationships, friend requests, and direct messages are stored in Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Server configuration: Server settings (port, RAM, MOTD, auto-start preferences) are stored locally on your device using your operating system's application data directory. This data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Mod and modpack preferences: Your installed mods, mod profiles, and modpack selections are stored locally in your browser's localStorage within the application. This data does not leave your device.
  • Usage analytics: We do not collect behavioral analytics, crash telemetry, or usage statistics. The launcher does not phone home other than to check for updates on GitHub Releases.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the core features of Voxel Client, including launching Minecraft, managing mods, and running servers.
  • To authenticate you and maintain your session across launcher restarts.
  • To enable social features including friends, direct messages, and global chat.
  • To display your profile, rank, and cosmetics to other users of the Service.
  • To deliver software updates via GitHub Releases and the electron-updater library.
  • To respond to support requests sent to our email address.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond the operation of the Service.

4. Data Storage and Security

User account data is stored on Firebase, a platform provided by Google LLC, located in data centers within the United States and other regions as determined by Google. By using the Service, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and processed in these regions.

We implement reasonable security measures including Firebase Security Rules to restrict database access, HTTPS for all data in transit, and hashed password storage via Firebase Authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Locally stored data (Microsoft tokens, server config, mod preferences) is stored in your operating system's user application data directory and is protected by your operating system's standard user account permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

Voxel Client integrates with the following third-party services. Each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, Realtime Database, and Firestore. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft / Mojang: Microsoft account authentication for online Minecraft play. Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Modrinth: Mod and modpack metadata is fetched from the Modrinth public API. No personal data is sent to Modrinth. Modrinth Privacy Policy
  • GitHub: Update metadata is fetched from GitHub Releases. No personal data is sent to GitHub during the update check. GitHub Privacy Statement

6. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at jordanengrashes@gmail.com and we will process the deletion within 30 days.

Chat messages may be retained for up to 90 days for moderation purposes before being automatically purged.

Locally stored data (Microsoft tokens, preferences, server config) can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the application or manually clearing the application data directory.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete such information.

Minecraft itself is rated E10+ (Everyone 10 and older) by the ESRB. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their children's use of the Service and any communication features including Global Chat and Direct Messages.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data (right to be forgotten).
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jordanengrashes@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The Voxel Client desktop application uses Electron's localStorage API to store your preferences (mod selections, server settings, UI preferences) locally on your device. This data is not shared with us or any third party. No tracking cookies are used.

The Voxel Client website (this site) does not use cookies or tracking scripts of any kind.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For significant changes, we will make a reasonable effort to notify users via the application or the GitHub repository.
